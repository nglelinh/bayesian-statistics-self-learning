Chapter 12: Bayesian Hands-on Labs

Chapter 12 collects hands-on labs so you can turn the course's theoretical knowledge into concrete analysis in a notebook. If the earlier chapters helped you build concepts and intuition, this chapter is where you practice reading a problem, setting up a model, computing, interpreting the results, and checking your own solution.

The labs follow a review path from conditional probability, Bayes' rule, and simulation, through conjugate models, point estimation, and mixture priors, to the Bayesian regression workflow. Each lab comes with both the assignment and the solution, so you can work through it yourself and compare approaches when needed.

The goal of the chapter is to help you learn Bayes through guided practice. The content of this page is synchronized from the lab notebooks, so when the practice materials are updated, the chapter will reflect the latest version of the exercises.
